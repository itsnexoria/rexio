    Macros

    Records global mouse and keyboard input while active — click Record, perform the actions, then Stop. Assign a hotkey or pixel-color trigger to play a macro back anywhere; pressing the hotkey again mid-playback stops it. Press Ctrl+Shift+Esc anytime to release all keys/buttons a macro is holding and stop playback.

    Running under Wayland — global recording and hotkeys may not work depending on your compositor. X11/XWayland is the supported path for now.
